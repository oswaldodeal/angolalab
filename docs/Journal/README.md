# AEIS

## Angola Economic Intelligence System

**Version:** 1.0 (Living Documentation)

---

## What is AEIS?

The Angola Economic Intelligence System (AEIS) is the analytical engine that powers AngolaLab.

Its purpose is not simply to collect information.

Its purpose is to transform verified information into structured intelligence that supports better decisions for businesses, institutions, researchers, students, investors and policymakers.

AEIS combines systems thinking, economic intelligence, business intelligence and human verification to identify opportunities, risks and structural change.

---

## Our Mission

To help people understand not only **what is happening**, but also:

* Why it is happening.
* What systems are producing it.
* What opportunities or risks may emerge.
* What actions deserve attention.

---

## Core Philosophy

AEIS does not analyse isolated events.

AEIS studies the structures beneath those events.

Every observation is treated as part of a larger economic, social or institutional system.

---

## The AEIS Principles

1. Evidence before opinion.
2. Structure before headlines.
3. Alignment before conclusion.
4. Opportunity alongside risk.
5. Human judgement before publication.
6. Continuous learning.
7. Transparency through documented standards.

---

## Institutional Standards

AEIS is supported by the following standards:

* AEIS Analytical Framework
* AEIS Presentation Standard (AEIS PS)
* AEIS Intelligence Score
* Verified Profile Standard
* Data Reliability Framework

Each standard evolves through version control as AngolaLab grows.

---

## Vision

To become a trusted intelligence institution that helps Angola and other developing economies make better decisions through transparent, evidence-based systems thinking.
