# AEIS Intelligence Score

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The AEIS Intelligence Score provides a structured method for communicating the overall confidence and strategic significance of an intelligence assessment.

The score does not predict the future.

It summarises the quality of available evidence, the strength of structural relationships and the practical value of the analysis.

---

# Objectives

The AEIS Intelligence Score exists to:

* Improve transparency.
* Communicate confidence.
* Standardise analytical reporting.
* Support consistent decision-making.
* Allow comparison between intelligence briefs.

---

# Assessment Categories

Every intelligence assessment may consider the following dimensions.

## Evidence Quality

How reliable and verifiable are the available sources?

---

## Structural Importance

Does the event represent a temporary occurrence or a meaningful structural change?

---

## Opportunity Potential

Does the information indicate business, investment, educational or institutional opportunities?

---

## Risk Significance

What level of economic, operational or strategic risk is identified?

---

## Confidence Level

How confident is AEIS that the current assessment reflects reality?

Confidence should always acknowledge uncertainty where appropriate.

---

# Interpretation

Higher scores indicate stronger analytical confidence and greater strategic relevance.

Lower scores do not necessarily indicate poor quality.

They may simply reflect limited evidence or higher uncertainty.

---

# Principles

The AEIS Intelligence Score should never replace detailed analysis.

It exists to support understanding, not simplify complex reality.

The score should always be interpreted alongside the accompanying Intelligence Brief.

---

# Human Responsibility

The Intelligence Score supports analysts.

It does not replace analytical judgement.

Final publication remains the responsibility of AngolaLab analysts.

---

# Relationship with AEIS

The Intelligence Score is generated using the AEIS Analytical Framework and communicated through the AEIS Presentation Standard.

Together they ensure transparency, consistency and institutional trust.
