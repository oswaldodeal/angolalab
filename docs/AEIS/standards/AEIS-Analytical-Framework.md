# AEIS Analytical Framework

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The AEIS Analytical Framework defines the thinking process used by AngolaLab to transform data into intelligence.

Its objective is not simply to report facts, but to understand the structures that produce those facts.

Every AEIS analysis follows the same disciplined methodology to ensure consistency, transparency and long-term trust.

---

# The AEIS Thinking Process

## Step 1 — Observation

Collect verified information.

Examples:

* Government statistics
* Verified company profiles
* Economic indicators
* Tourism reports
* Business reports
* Market data
* Infrastructure developments

At this stage AEIS does **not** interpret.

It only observes.

---

## Step 2 — Validation

Before any analysis begins, information is evaluated using the Data Reliability Framework.

Questions include:

* Is the source reliable?
* Can the information be independently verified?
* Is the information current?
* Are multiple sources aligned?

If confidence is insufficient, no conclusion is produced.

---

## Step 3 — Pattern Detection

AEIS searches for relationships rather than isolated events.

Examples include:

* Tourism growth and employment.
* Infrastructure and investment.
* Manufacturing and imports.
* Agriculture and logistics.
* Education and workforce demand.

The objective is to identify recurring patterns.

---

## Step 4 — Structural Analysis

Rather than asking:

"What happened?"

AEIS asks:

"What structure produced this outcome?"

This stage examines economic, institutional and social relationships to understand the underlying drivers.

---

## Step 5 — Opportunity Assessment

Every major change creates opportunities as well as risks.

AEIS identifies:

* Business opportunities
* Investment opportunities
* Workforce opportunities
* Education opportunities
* Provincial development opportunities

---

## Step 6 — Human Verification

No intelligence is published automatically.

AEIS may generate observations and draft analyses, but every published conclusion requires analyst review.

This principle protects the credibility of AngolaLab.

---

## Step 7 — Intelligence Publication

Only after successful verification is an Intelligence Brief, Journal article, Business Report or Presentation released.

Every publication follows AngolaLab standards.

---

# Core Principles

* Evidence before opinion.
* Structure before headlines.
* Alignment before conclusion.
* Opportunity alongside risk.
* Human judgement before publication.
* Continuous learning.
* Transparency through documented standards.

---

# Philosophy

AEIS does not compete to publish first.

AEIS seeks to understand first.

The quality of intelligence depends not on speed, but on disciplined observation, verification and structural thinking.

---

# Relationship with AngolaLab

AEIS is the analytical engine.

AngolaLab is the platform through which that intelligence is shared with businesses, institutions, researchers, students and the public.

Every AngolaLab product is ultimately powered by the AEIS Analytical Framework.
