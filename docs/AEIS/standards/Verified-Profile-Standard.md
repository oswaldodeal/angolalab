# Verified Profile Standard

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Verified Profile Standard defines how AngolaLab evaluates and verifies organisations listed within the platform.

Its purpose is to increase transparency, improve trust and provide users with structured information about businesses, institutions and organisations operating within Angola.

Verification does not represent endorsement.

It confirms that available information has been reviewed according to AEIS standards.

---

# Objectives

The Verified Profile Standard exists to:

* Improve transparency.
* Encourage accurate business information.
* Support informed decision-making.
* Increase confidence in company profiles.
* Promote continuous improvement.

---

# Information Categories

A verified profile may include:

* Organisation name.
* Sector.
* Province.
* Contact information.
* Official website.
* Number of employees.
* Staff currently required.
* Years in operation.
* Products or services.
* Export markets.
* Certifications (where applicable).

---

# Verification Principles

Verification should consider:

* Identity confirmation.
* Publicly available information.
* Official registration where appropriate.
* Information consistency.
* Periodic review and updates.

Verification confirms available information at the time of assessment.

It does not guarantee future performance.

---

# Quality Indicators

Where appropriate, AEIS may publish structured indicators relating to:

* Service quality.
* Operational transparency.
* Customer information.
* Workforce development.
* Environmental initiatives.
* Community engagement.

These indicators should be evidence-based and regularly reviewed.

---

# Independence

Verified status shall never be purchased.

Commercial relationships shall not influence verification outcomes.

Verification decisions remain independent of sponsorship, advertising or membership.

---

# Relationship with AEIS

Verified Profiles provide structured organisational data that supports Business Intelligence, Workforce Intelligence, Tourism Intelligence and Opportunity Intelligence.

---

# Living Documentation

This document forms part of the Living Documentation of the Angola Economic Intelligence System (AEIS).

It reflects the current institutional standard and will evolve continuously as AngolaLab grows, new knowledge emerges and analytical methodologies mature.

Version history shall preserve the evolution of AEIS while maintaining transparency and institutional consistency.
