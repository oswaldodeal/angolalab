# Purpose

This document establishes the institutional philosophy of AngolaLab.

It defines what AngolaLab is, what it exists to achieve, and the principles that guide every laboratory, publication, research project, and digital platform developed under its name.

---

# Institutional Principle

> **AngolaLab is an institution.**

The website is one interface through which its research, education, laboratories, and publications are made accessible.

Every digital feature should strengthen the institution rather than define it.

---

# Mission

AngolaLab exists to develop independent thinkers through research, education, systems thinking, and continuous learning.

The institution seeks to organise knowledge, strengthen decision-making, and encourage evidence-based understanding across disciplines.

---

# Foundation

AngolaLab is built upon five interconnected pillars.

- Philosophy
- Research
- Education
- Intelligence
- Innovation

Each pillar strengthens the others.

No individual project exists in isolation.

---

# The Laboratory

Every AngolaLab laboratory represents an environment for structured observation and learning.

Laboratories are designed to investigate relationships, organise knowledge, and communicate evidence rather than opinion.

Examples include:

- Trading Intelligence
- Tourism Intelligence
- BODIVA Laboratory
- Future AEIS Laboratories

---

# The Website

The AngolaLab website is not the institution.

Its purpose is to provide public access to AngolaLab's research, educational resources, laboratories, publications, and institutional activities.

The website serves the institution.

The institution does not serve the website.

---

# Research Before Technology

Technology is a tool.

Research provides direction.

Every technological decision should strengthen AngolaLab's ability to educate, investigate, organise, and communicate knowledge.

---

# Long-Term Vision

AngolaLab is designed to grow through continuous refinement rather than rapid expansion.

New laboratories, publications, and technologies should emerge naturally from the institution's philosophy and research.

Growth should strengthen coherence rather than increase complexity.

---

# Official Principle

> **Build the institution first. Build the interface second.**
