# Data Reliability Framework

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Data Reliability Framework establishes how AEIS evaluates the reliability of information before it is used in analysis.

Reliable intelligence begins with reliable evidence.

Every dataset, report or observation should be assessed before contributing to any AEIS publication.

---

# Objectives

The framework exists to:

* Improve analytical integrity.
* Increase transparency.
* Reduce misinformation.
* Communicate uncertainty.
* Strengthen institutional trust.

---

# Assessment Principles

AEIS considers the following questions:

* Who produced the information?
* Is the source identifiable?
* Can the information be independently verified?
* Is the information current?
* Does supporting evidence exist?
* Are multiple sources consistent?

---

# Reliability Levels

Information may be classified as:

**High Reliability**

Multiple independent sources confirm the information.

---

**Moderate Reliability**

Evidence is generally consistent but requires continued monitoring.

---

**Limited Reliability**

Evidence exists but remains incomplete or partially verified.

---

**Insufficient Reliability**

Available information is inadequate for analytical conclusions.

AEIS should clearly communicate this uncertainty.

---

# Transparency

When uncertainty exists, AEIS should communicate uncertainty rather than imply certainty.

Missing information should be reported where relevant.

Analysts should distinguish clearly between:

* Verified facts.
* Analytical interpretation.
* Assumptions.
* Areas requiring further investigation.

---

# Independence

The reliability assessment shall remain independent of political, commercial or institutional influence.

Evidence shall determine confidence.

---

# Relationship with AEIS

The Data Reliability Framework operates before the AEIS Analytical Framework.

Reliable evidence forms the foundation upon which all intelligence is built.

---

# Closing Principle

Trust is earned through consistency, transparency and disciplined verification.

Every published intelligence brief reflects not only analytical skill but also respect for evidence.
