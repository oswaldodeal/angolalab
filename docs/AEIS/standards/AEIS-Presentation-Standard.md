# AEIS Presentation Standard (AEIS PS)

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The AEIS Presentation Standard defines how AngolaLab communicates intelligence.

Its purpose is to ensure that every briefing, presentation, report and public communication follows a consistent structure, regardless of the subject or audience.

Consistency improves clarity, trust and institutional identity.

---

# Philosophy

AEIS presentations are designed to explain, not persuade.

They seek to increase understanding through structured evidence rather than emotional appeal.

Every presentation should allow the audience to distinguish clearly between observation, analysis and recommendation.

---

# Standard Structure

Every AEIS presentation should follow the same logical sequence.

## 1. Subject

Clearly define the topic under analysis.

---

## 2. Executive Summary

Provide a concise overview of the key findings.

---

## 3. Observation

Present verified facts without interpretation.

---

## 4. Assessment

Explain the structural relationships identified by the AEIS Analytical Framework.

---

## 5. Opportunities and Risks

Identify potential business, economic, educational or institutional implications.

Both opportunities and risks should be presented where relevant.

---

## 6. Recommendations

Provide practical considerations appropriate to the intended audience.

Recommendations should distinguish between evidence-based conclusions and areas requiring further monitoring.

---

## 7. AEIS Intelligence Score

Summarise the overall analytical assessment using the AEIS Intelligence Score framework.

---

# Communication Principles

Every AEIS presentation should be:

* Evidence-based.
* Structured.
* Transparent.
* Neutral.
* Accessible.
* Visually consistent.

---

# Audience

The same presentation standard may be adapted for:

* Government institutions
* Businesses
* Universities
* Researchers
* Investors
* Journalists
* Public audiences

The structure remains constant while the level of technical detail may vary.

---

# Relationship with the AEIS Analytical Framework

The AEIS Presentation Standard communicates the conclusions generated by the AEIS Analytical Framework.

The framework determines how intelligence is produced.

The presentation standard determines how that intelligence is communicated.

Both standards operate together to maintain consistency across AngolaLab.

---

# Closing Principle

A presentation should never seek to impress through complexity.

Its purpose is to improve understanding through clarity, discipline and evidence.
