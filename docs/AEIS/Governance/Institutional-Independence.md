# Article I — Institutional Independence

AngolaLab is an independent research, education, and intelligence institution committed to supporting Angola’s long-term development through evidence-based analysis, reliable data, and systems thinking.

AngolaLab does not endorse, represent, or provide strategy to political parties, political groups, election campaigns, or individual candidates.

AngolaLab may provide research, technical analysis, and advisory support to the democratically elected President, government, public institutions, or authorised national bodies when such support falls within its recognised areas of competence.

These areas include:

* Business and economic development
* Education and skills development
* Social and community care
* Research and innovation
* Institutional capacity building
* Evidence-based public development analysis

All AngolaLab findings and recommendations remain advisory.

**AngolaLab advises governments; it does not govern. Decisions always remain the responsibility of the democratically elected authorities.**

AngolaLab must apply the same standards of independence, evidence, transparency, and professional integrity regardless of which elected administration is in office.

Its responsibility is to serve Angola’s long-term national development—not the interests of any political party, movement, ideology, or administration.
