# Editorial Independence Charter

**Angola Economic Intelligence System (AEIS)**

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Editorial Independence Charter defines the principles that protect the integrity, neutrality and credibility of AngolaLab and the Angola Economic Intelligence System (AEIS).

The purpose of this Charter is to ensure that every publication, analysis and intelligence brief is guided by evidence, transparent methodology and professional judgement.

Trust is the foundation upon which AngolaLab is built.

---

# Institutional Commitment

AngolaLab exists to improve understanding through evidence-based intelligence.

The organisation does not exist to promote political parties, commercial interests or institutional agendas.

Its responsibility is to pursue truthful, transparent and disciplined analysis in the public interest.

---

# Editorial Principles

Every publication shall follow these principles:

* Evidence before opinion.
* Structure before headlines.
* Alignment before conclusion.
* Transparency before certainty.
* Opportunity alongside risk.
* Human judgement before publication.

---

# Independence

Editorial decisions shall remain independent of:

* Political influence.
* Commercial influence.
* Sponsorship.
* Advertising.
* Financial contributions.
* Personal relationships.

No external organisation shall determine the analytical conclusions published by AngolaLab.

---

# Evidence

AEIS follows evidence wherever it leads.

Positive findings shall be published.

Negative findings shall be published.

Uncertainty shall be published.

Missing information shall be acknowledged.

Evidence shall never be selectively presented to favour predetermined conclusions.

---

# Sponsorship

Commercial partnerships are permitted.

Editorial influence is not.

Sponsors do not purchase:

* favourable analysis;
* higher Intelligence Scores;
* Verified Profile status;
* positive recommendations;
* editorial approval.

Commercial relationships shall remain separate from analytical judgement.

---

# Governments and Public Institutions

AngolaLab may analyse public policy, institutions and government activity using the same analytical standards applied to every other organisation.

Respectful engagement does not compromise editorial independence.

No government shall possess editorial authority over AEIS publications.

---

# Corrections

If an error is identified, AngolaLab shall correct it transparently.

Corrections strengthen trust.

They do not weaken it.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence may assist research, organisation and analytical drafting.

Final editorial responsibility always remains with AngolaLab analysts.

No publication shall be released solely because it was generated by artificial intelligence.

---

# Public Trust

Trust is earned through:

* consistency;
* transparency;
* methodological discipline;
* intellectual honesty;
* continuous improvement.

Every publication contributes to the long-term reputation of AngolaLab.

---

# Our Commitment

AngolaLab does not compete to publish first.

AngolaLab seeks to understand first.

The institution values long-term credibility above short-term attention.

---

# Closing Statement

Evidence has no political affiliation.

Truth requires discipline.

Trust requires independence.

These principles shall guide AngolaLab for future generations.

---

# Living Documentation

This Charter forms part of the Living Documentation of the Angola Economic Intelligence System (AEIS).

It will evolve responsibly as AngolaLab grows while preserving its commitment to editorial independence, institutional integrity and public trust.
